Call Scorecards

Every finished voice-agent call, graded the same way a call center grades a human agent

← Voice agent demo

Same 4 buckets a call-center QA scorecard grades a human agent on. Outcome and Economics & Reliability are deterministic - read straight off the session, no LLM involved. Customer Experience (CSAT/CES) and Compliance are judgment calls, so they come from a post-call LLM-as-judge pass over the transcript + the tool-call log (Claude Haiku 4.5 - see src/scoring/judge.ts). Cost is an estimate against Deepgram's published per-minute pricing, not an invoice reconciliation.

Outcome - did the job get done (FCR) Economics & Reliability - speed, cost, uptime Customer Experience - CSAT + CES (effort) Compliance - accurate, on-brand
